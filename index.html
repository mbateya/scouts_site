Living the Scout Law with Islamic Values

A Scout is Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly, Courteous, Kind, Obedient, Cheerful, Thrifty, Brave, Clean, and Reverent. Each of these values aligns beautifully with Islamic principles.
